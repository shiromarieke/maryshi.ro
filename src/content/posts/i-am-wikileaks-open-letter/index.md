In 2017, I coordinated the open letter asking US president Trump to immediately close the Grand Jury investigation into WikiLeaks and drop any charges planned by the US Department of Justice against all WikiLeaks staff members. The open letter was initially signed by Edward Snowden, Noam Chomsky, Ken Loach, Yanis Varoufakis and Vivienne Westwood, among others.

Read the open letter [here](https://defend.wikileaks.org/openletter/), and the Guardian coverage [here](https://www.theguardian.com/media/2017/may/15/edward-snowden-assange-wikileaks-open-letter).
